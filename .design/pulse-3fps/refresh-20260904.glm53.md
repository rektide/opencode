- Resolution (the prescribed one, semantics preserved: schema-valid numbers are

  `enabled={(local.animate ?? true) && config.animations !== false}`
- Committed as `sumozrksmpol` / `3d84d7d6baf3` "fix(tui): keep fade-in-text
  enabled gate boolean under numeric animations".
