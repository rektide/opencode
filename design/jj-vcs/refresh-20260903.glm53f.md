## #46684 disposition — mechanical, not supersession

`4beaffbda958` generalizes the app review-diff gates from
`project.vcs === "git"` to truthy `project.vcs` (any backend), and deletes
the `vcs: project.vcs === "git" ? "git" : undefined` downgrade in
`global-sync/utils.ts` so every backend's label passes through. Touched:
`session/review/model.ts`, `global-sync/utils.ts` + its test, and
`session/screen.tsx`.

Our C4 (`feat(app): review Jujutsu working-copy changes`, `631d6d2de0b4`)
widened the exact same gates, narrowly (`=== "git" || === "jj"`), and
pass-through `vcs: project.vcs`. Upstream's truthy form strictly contains
ours: for jj projects the base's behavior is identical to C4's — review
options, vcs/details queries, and screen diffs all open. One-sentence test:
upstream wanted *all* backends to reach review diffs; the feature wanted jj
projects to reach review diffs; the base's generalized gates preserve both,
so there is no choice to make and no jj behavior lost. The jj-ness of review
lives server-side in our C3 provider (`vcs/jj.ts` status/diff/info), which
#46684 does not touch.

Bonus: #46684 changes `noGit` from `vcs !== "git"` to `!vcs`, which **fixes
the known follow-up** recorded in [`rewrite1.glm53.md`](rewrite1.glm53.md)
("noGit memo reads `!== "git"` while jj projects offer the git review mode")
— upstream closed our latent inconsistency for us.

Resolution: C4's two files restored to the base version inside its
duplicate → C4 empty → abandoned. The line drops from 11 to 10 commits.
Flagged to the operator as a structural change, though it removes no
capability: everything C4 opened for jj ships in the baseline now.
